It surfaces answers, guidance, and recommendations exactly when needed to help reps prepare, execute, and follow up faster.
Works in: Gmail, Outlook, Salesforce Opportunities, Chrome, Slack, Gong, Chorus, Salesloft, Outreach, and more.

- Change Management Notifications: Alerts for new content, process updates, or product changes.
